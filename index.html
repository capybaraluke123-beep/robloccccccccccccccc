01 WHAT IS BUILD DAY?

Build Day is an online Roblox hackathon for anyone who has ever thought, “I could make a game like that.”

Learn the basics of Roblox Studio, turn your idea into a real experience, and share it with the community. No experience required. Just curiosity, creativity, and a little competitive energy.
